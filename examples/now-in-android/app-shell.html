What it does

MainActivity installs the splash screen before super.onCreate, and keeps it up (setKeepOnScreenCondition) until MainActivityViewModel.uiState leaves Loading for Success — the first userData emission decides the real theme (brand, dark mode, dynamic color) before a single themed pixel is drawn, and that themeSettings is then tracked as its own mutableStateOf so a system dark-mode change recomposes independently of the view-model. NiaApp builds three bottom-nav items — For You, Bookmarks, Interests — each with its own back stack (NavigationState.subStacks); tapping a different tab only ever switches which sub-stack NavDisplay shows, tapping the current tab again clears that tab's stack back to its root, and Search/Topic/Settings push onto whichever tab's stack was active when they opened. A tab's icon gets a small notification dot while it has unread news (topLevelNavKeysWithUnreadResources, recomputed from both the For You feed and the bookmarks list). On a wide screen, Interests and Topic render side by side instead of one pushing over the other (ListDetailSceneStrategy).
