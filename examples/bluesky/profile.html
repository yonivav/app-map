Profile Module

← App map · A profile — header with follow and edit, and a pager of up to nine tabs, from Posts and Replies to Feeds, Lists and Starter Packs.

What it does

Profiles open by handle or DID: ProfileScreenInner resolves the handle to a DID (useResolveDidQuery), loads the profile (useProfileQuery), and renders ProfileScreenLoaded once both are there; a placeholder from a feed item can show at once while the full profile loads. Content that moderation hides is wrapped in a ScreenHider you can choose to look past. ProfileHeader shows the banner, avatar, names, bio, counts, and Follow, Edit profile or Subscribe for labelers. Below it, PagerWithHeader holds only the tabs that apply: Labels and Lists for a labeler, then Posts, Replies, Media, Videos, Likes (your own profile), Feeds, Starter Packs and Lists when the account has any. The post tabs are ProfileFeedSections around the same PostFeed as Home, with an author|… or likes|… feed descriptor, so they page and cache the same way. A floating button opens the composer, prefilled with a mention when you're on someone else's profile.
